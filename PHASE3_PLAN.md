## 3. The generalisation experiment (RQ3)

### 3.1 What is being asked

Kitsios et al. (ASE 2025) already established the headline: re-evaluated on **unseen**
functionality, task-specific models lose **up to 48%, on average 31%** F1, while LLMs lose ~3%.
That result is *not* the contribution here and must be cited, not re-discovered (`SCOPE.md` Part 5).
The question this phase answers is narrower and is the deck's third expected result:

> **Does the size of the drop differ across negative-selection strategies?**
> Δ = F1_seen − F1_unseen, per condition, as a mean over the three seeds **with the spread
> visible**.

If Δ is statistically indistinguishable across C1/C2/C3, that is a finding ("on this corpus, with
these labels, negative hardness does not change the unseen-functionality drop"), not a failure.
No significance tests, p-values or multiple-comparison corrections — they are out of scope
(`SCOPE.md` Part 5); report means, spreads and the per-seed values.

### 3.2 Protocol (Route A: hold out whole functionalities)

- Hold out **k = `SPRIME_HELDOUT_K` = 3** functionalities; train on the remaining **20**.
- **"Best-represented" must be a measured rule, not an assumption** (D4). s′ is described as
  functionality-*balanced*, but the per-functionality pair counts have not been recorded in this
  repo. Print them, commit the counts, and state the selection rule (highest pair count, ties
  broken by functionality id) before looking at any F1.
- Report **F1_seen** (pairs whose functionality was trained on), **F1_unseen** (the three held-out
  functionalities) and **Δ**, per condition, per seed, then mean + spread across seeds 13/14/15.
- **C0 is evaluated once** on the same seen/unseen partition, exactly as in Phase 2 — same
  tokenizer, pooling, truncation and scoring path, no fine-tuning.
- The holdout is **the same three functionalities for every condition and every seed**. A different
  holdout per condition would confound strategy with difficulty.

### 3.3 Where the negatives come from — the one genuinely open design question (D3)

s′ is a **labelled pair** dataset (`code1, code2, label, functionality_id`). It has no anchor
stream and no mined negative pool, and Phase 2's conditions are defined by *how negatives are
mined from the CodeXGLUE train corpus*. "Train on s′" therefore needs an explicit choice:

| | What it does | Cost | What Δ then means |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(A) Evaluate the Phase 2 checkpoints on s′** | no new training; score the nine existing models on seen/unseen | ~0 GPU-h | transfer of a CodeXGLUE-trained model, not training on s′ |
| **(B) Fine-tune on s′, mining negatives from s′ itself** — C1 random non-clone partner, C2 BM25-nearest non-clone, C3 cosine-nearest non-clone, all inside the 20 held-in functionalities | one encode pass over s′'s fragments (≤ 9,200 texts) + 9 short runs | a few GPU-h | the intended reading: the same manipulation, on a corpus with functionality labels |
| **(C) Fine-tune on the Phase 2 triples, excluding held-out functionality** | — | — | **not possible**: CodeXGLUE fragments carry no functionality ids — recovering them is "the single largest unbudgeted task in the plan" (`FINAL_SPEC.md` §0.1), and `GROUND_TRUTH.md` puts it plainly: "you probably cannot recover functionality IDs for the CodeXGLUE fragments cheaply" |

**Recommended: (B)**, because it is the only option that keeps *negative strategy* as the
independent variable while training on functionality-labelled data — with (A) recorded as a
free secondary reading if the runs are cheap. (B) obliges entry criterion 4 (hardness check on the
s′ negatives) and a recorded `VERSION`-level note that the generalisation runs use a different
corpus from the main table, so the two tables are **not** directly comparable in absolute F1.

### 3.4 Budget

s′ is 4,600 pairs total, so the training cap must be **smaller** than the main 32,000 — the spec's
"six smaller runs" language is deliberate; **do not normalise the generalisation runs to the main
cap** (`CLOUD.md` §5.6). Record the chosen cap in `settings.py` next to `SPRIME_HELDOUT_K` and keep
it identical across conditions and seeds. If quota runs out, the ladder is `SCOPE.md` P6-8: cut
**seeds** before cutting **strategies** (3 → 2 seeds before C3 disappears); "complete weak beats
incomplete strong".

### 3.5 Noise

A 3-functionality holdout out of 23 on 4,600 pairs will be noisy, and the audit already puts a
36% label-noise floor under every negative. Per `SCOPE.md` P6-6: **if the numbers are wildly
noisy, report the noise.** Do not add holdout folds until the variance goes away. Leave-one-
functionality-out rotation (mean and range of Δ across folds) is the first SHOULD if time allows
(`SCOPE.md` Part 3.2) — it is an explicit upgrade, not a rescue.

## 4. The figure (UMAP)

- **Four panels — C0, C1, C2, C3** — with a **fixed seed and fixed `n_neighbors` / `min_dist`**,
  both recorded in `settings.py` (`SCOPE.md` P2-18).
- **The same sample of fragments in every panel.** Different samples per panel make the panels
  incomparable and the figure meaningless.
- **Colour by functionality, including the held-out ones**, so the unseen-functionality story is
  visible in the same picture as the Δ table.
- **A figure, not evidence.** It ships *after* the table (FINAL_SPEC correction 8) and no claim in
  the write-up may rest on it.
- Writes to `report/figures/` (created by this phase; generated, never hand-edited).
- **Dependency gap:** `umap-learn==0.5.6` is already in `settings.PINNED`, but no plotting library
  is. `embeded/tests/test_env_pins.py` requires the three pin sources to agree exactly, so adding
  one means editing **`settings.PINNED`, `embeded/requirements.txt` and the notebook install cell
  together** — the test is what enforces that promise, and it also rejects any `torch==` pin.

## 5. The demo (`demo/app.py`)

One file, Gradio (`gradio==5.49.1`, already pinned), no serving infrastructure (`SCOPE.md` Part 5
excludes it — the demo is a local single-file app, not a deployment).

- **Default view: real held-out test pairs with their gold labels** (`SCOPE.md` P2-21). Not
  synthetic examples, not a blank text box.
- **Per-pair display:** `gold` / `C0 cosine vs its threshold` / `C_best cosine vs its threshold`,
  with the threshold drawn as a **visible marker on the score bar** — the reader must be able to
  see *why* the decision came out as it did.
- **All four outcome modes rendered.** ⚠ The phrase appears in `SCOPE.md` P2-21 and
  `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §4 but **is never enumerated anywhere in the repo**. This plan reads it
  as the 2 × 2 of (C0 decision) × (C_best decision) — both right, C0 wrong / C_best right, C0 right
  / C_best wrong, both wrong — and requires **at least one real example pair available in the
  picker for each cell**. Confirm or replace that reading and record it (D6) before building.
- **Free-text paste stays, labelled *"illustrative only — not part of the evaluation"***, in those
  words.
- Loads from the committed run metadata plus `predictions.npz` / `checkpoint.pt` — it must not
  train, and must not re-encode the corpus at import time. Checkpoints live outside Git, so the
  demo's first action is `python -m scripts.hf_artifacts pull --if-configured`; say so in its
  header comment.

## 7. Risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Δ differences are within seed noise | Report mean **and** per-seed values; P6-6 forbids adding folds to make it go away; LOFO is a declared SHOULD, not a fix |
| The manipulation does not exist on s′ (C1 ≈ C2 ≈ C3 hardness) | Entry criterion 4: run the hardness check on the s′ negatives *before* training, with the same `evaluate_gap` |
| Comparing the generalisation table to the main table as if they shared a corpus | They do not (D3). Say so in both table captions |
| Temptation to add a fourth strategy or rescue C3 with a lower LR | `SCOPE.md` P6-1, P6-2. "No difference here" is a publishable finding |
| GPU quota | P6-8 ladder: seeds before strategies; generalisation is secondary to the main table |
| Overclaiming | `SCOPE.md` Part 5's list, verbatim: *"agrees with BigCloneBench's labels at F1 = X"*, never "detects semantic clones"; the generalisation test is **not** novel — cite Kitsios et al.; no claim beyond Java/BigCloneBench; no claim to have separated hard negatives from false negatives (we *measured* the rate) |
| Label noise inflating the unseen drop | The 36% pooled false-negative floor applies to the negatives, not to s′'s gold labels; keep the two separate and say which is which |
